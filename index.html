Research

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing and Multilingual Transfer Learning, with an emphasis on low-resource language settings. Current and recent threads include:

  • Ideological stance and political polarization detection in Bangla social media discourse (IEEE SPICSCON'26, under review)
  • Hybrid transformer–sequential models for claim checkworthiness and sentiment classification from news headlines (in preparation)
  • Fine-tuning transformer models (BanglaBERT) for Bangla sentiment analysis
  • Applied transfer learning for agriculture and environment: crop disease/variety classification (ECCT'26, JBAS'26) and XAI-based air quality forecasting with health-optimized routing (undergraduate thesis)
